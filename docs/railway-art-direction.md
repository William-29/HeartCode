# Railway illustrations

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool during the live playthrough. These replace the flat railway scene drawings; original assets elsewhere in the game were retained. The selected PNGs were copied into the project, without visual post-processing.

Each 1536 × 1024 illustration is displayed as a responsive, cropped landscape view. Location names, captions and travel controls remain readable HTML outside the artwork. Arrival has a short settling animation and respects the existing motion controls.

## Final prompt set

Each call used the following shared prompt, followed by its location scene:

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: wide landscape game location backdrop, no UI. Match a cozy detailed hand-painted pixel-art adventure village: warm 1950s European back alleys, tiled roofs, ivy, flowers, worn stone and wood, amber lanterns, soft cast shadows, inviting layers of depth, rich color variation with calm readable composition. Landscape 3:2 composition. No characters, no words, labels, borders, watermarks or modern technology. This is a complete new location illustration for Hearthcode, a gentle Python-learning game.

**Hearthwick:** A small rural village railway station at dusk. A green and burgundy vintage steam train waits at a cobbled platform. Warm station windows, a small ticket office with striped awning, oak benches, flowers in terracotta pots, brass lamps, tracks across the lower part, countryside and distant cozy rooftops. Welcoming and intimate, generous open platform, no grand monumental architecture.

**Brinehaven:** Brinehaven, a cozy seaside railway stop and harbour town. A little stone ticket office and striped awnings, small fishing boats and ferry moored in turquoise harbor, warm cafe windows, brass lamps, flower pots and a weathered wooden bench. Sunset apricot light and teal sea, a winding cobbled promenade in front, quaint terracotta roofs. An intimate village with the same handcrafted style.

**Snowbell:** Snowbell, a cozy snowy mountain village railway stop at blue hour. Snow-capped timber cottages, warm glowing windows, tiny station hut with kettle in the window, iron lanterns reflecting on cobbles under gentle snow, spruce trees, a quiet platform in front. Amber, dusty rose, muted blue snow. A comforting winter place, not bleak or icy.

**Fernwood:** Fernwood, a welcoming forest academy courtyard reached by a rural railway. Ivy-covered old stone and timber study cottages under ancient trees, reading windows lit amber, a small glasshouse, flower beds and ferns, worn stone paths leading between books and growing plants, a quiet station halt at one edge. Soft late afternoon shafts of light, moss green, copper, gold. Intimate woodland learning place rather than a giant fantasy castle.
